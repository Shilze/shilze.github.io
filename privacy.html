Car Remote for Garmin - Privacy policy

Car Remote is a personal, non-commercial watch app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.

  • The watch app talks only to a backend server operated by the app's owner, using a revocable access token entered in the app settings.
  • The backend reads battery, range, charging and climate state of the owner's own vehicle, and sends climate start/stop commands when the user asks for them.
  • No location data is requested or stored. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Vehicle data is kept only as a short-lived cache. Tesla account tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server and can be removed at any time.
  • The app collects no analytics.
